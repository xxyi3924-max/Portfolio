About

Who I Am

I'm a first-year Engineering Science student at the University of Toronto (graduating 2029), pursuing a major in Biomedical Systems or Math-Statistics-Finance Engineering.

I enjoy building things that bridge disciplines — whether that's an AI agent that reads institutional trading signals before the crowd, a sip-and-puff assistive device for a wheelchair user, or a hackathon prototype that wins a creative design award.

I previously interned at Shenzhen YHLO Biotech as an R&D and Bioinformatics Engineer, where I applied machine learning to diagnose fevers of unknown origin. I hold two Chinese patents for firefighting drone systems.

Fluent in English and Mandarin. Currently based in Toronto.
